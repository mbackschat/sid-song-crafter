# SID Song Crafter: product purpose

The focus is to map a given musical score to SID in an inspiring, creative way that makes full use of the SID chip—or two chips in stereo—by choosing instruments, arrangements, articulation and effects.

The project builds and applies a reusable body of knowledge for making these interpretations. It connects musical intentions to SID techniques: which sounds, voicings, phrasing, modulation and spatial roles can express a song's character, under which constraints, and with what listening evidence. The library includes documented knowhow, editable instruments and arrangements, executable examples, reusable code and reproducible studies. It is more than a collection of presets or a rendering pipeline.

“SID” includes both MOS6581 and MOS8580, with single-SID and dual-SID stereo arrangements. Each deliverable declares its target model, clock and chip allocation; instrument choices are judged in that configuration.

## Musical scope

- Start from a supplied or sourced score, preserving its provenance and musical foundation. Original score composition is outside the current remit unless explicitly requested.
- Apply creativity through sound design and arrangement: instrument selection, voice allocation, register, balance, articulation, groove, effects and deliberate single- or dual-SID roles. Preserve source material separately from arrangement departures.
- Choose techniques for the intended mood and musical role. Making full use of SID means expressive control, including restraint; it does not require maximum loudness, constant activity or every available effect.
- Judge success by a compelling interpretation that respects the intended character. Correct notes, successful playback and a previously accepted instrument do not by themselves establish a suitable interpretation of another song.

## How the knowledge serves the product

Study existing SID music and its mechanisms, retain findings and listening verdicts, then apply and adapt them to another score. Each case should make later interpretations better informed and less laborious. Tools and automation serve this musical outcome; accumulating infrastructure or reproducing reference songs is not the final purpose.
