# Rambo native centered-lead evidence

The [native allocation](../../songs/studies/rambo-centered-native/README.md) duplicates the full source lead on A1/B1, retaining the original bass/arpeggio on A and inner/held support on B. B3 temporarily becomes the lead's source modulator where sync/ring require it. The [phrase verdict](../../songs/studies/rambo-centered-native/listening-review.json) accepts perceived centering, useful left/right space and fullness; the complete revised performance and ending remain under review. Music-derived descriptions and traces retain Martin Galway credit and the [loader's GPLv3 terms](../../samples/rambo-loader/README.md); reusable tools retain MIT separately.

The fresh 14-stage run took 220.366 seconds, its initial cached repeat 0.819 seconds. Independent review found that the compiler guards read the published mono SID without declaring it in that stage's cache inputs. The corrected recipe reran only those guards: 6.606 seconds total, 13 stages reused. The final repeat took 0.824 seconds with all 14 stages reused. Audio hashes remained unchanged. These timings exclude implementation, investigation and human listening.

The complete 11,000-call candidate matches 123,221 ordered stores and ten two-engine state checkpoints; peak play cost is 16,169/19,656 PAL cycles. VICE cadence error stays within seven cycles. Full stereo and mono-fold audio have no clipped samples, a surviving closing body and no alternating signal in the final second.

Equal lead-program trajectories and nearly equal standalone diagnostic power do not establish matching physical oscillator/envelope history or phase coherence. B3 previously played different material, and source-derived software entry-state patches cannot restore physical chip state. The accepted phrase establishes perceived centering in that context, not sample identity or a universal duplication recipe. Keep the full-song verdict separate.
