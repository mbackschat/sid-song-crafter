# Rambo: a separate palette interpretation

This is one sound-design interpretation of Martin Galway's *Rambo: First Blood Part II* loader. The [faithful independent reconstruction](../../../samples/rambo-loader/README.md) remains the reference. The candidate retains the complete [source graph](../../rambo-loader.json), melody, note calls, row durations, form, frequency table, Morse entrance and PAL MOS6581 target; it changes native instrument behavior. No new melody, harmony or percussion is added. The intended character is a singing foreground, a grounded bass ostinato and useful support that leaves room for the lead. **Human listening verdict: inconclusive; no improvement established.** See the [recorded feedback](listening-review.json). The candidate is parked while a separate stereo treatment preserves the accepted faithful palette.

The complete-song interpretation derives from Galway's GPLv3 music and uses the [same GPLv3 terms and attribution](../../../samples/rambo-loader/README.md). The candidate SID and audio remain local under ignored `build/` while review is pending; the [exact generated description](../../../evidence/rambo-interpretation/candidate.json) is retained as GPLv3 evidence. The [editable palette](palette.json) contains the musical intent, role choices and exact byte overrides; the [case-specific preparer](../../../scripts/studies/rambo/prepare-interpretation.py) applies them through the existing compiler, without replacing its player.

## Musical roles and deliberate departures

Executing the characteristic 32–48-second phrase identifies physical voice 1 as the higher lead/effects, voice 2 as the rapid bass ostinato and voice 3 as lower-root/harmonic support. They have 25, 82 and 61 note starts in that window respectively. Their roles change elsewhere in the complete source, so this is a unified palette treatment rather than a claim that every voice has one immutable orchestral role.

| Physical voice | Candidate treatment | Purpose and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1: lead/effects | Pulse body, AD `$26`, SR `$D9`; width `$650`, two 24-call PWM phases of `+$1C/-$1C`. Ordinary balanced 3/6/3-call vibrato uses `+/-16` frequency words after ten calls (about 200 ms). | Test a sustained, moving foreground. Only ordinary pitch cycles change: delay-slide flags, chord overlays, nonordinary frequency banks and explicit lead pitch/sound pokes retain their source values. These are raw frequency-word steps, not constant cents. |
| 2: bass ostinato | Broad pulse at `$700`, two 24-call phases of `+8/-8`; AD `$18`, SR `$B8`, seven-call release countdown. | Replace falling saw pitch with stable written pitch and a held body. This deliberately disables this voice's programmed frequency replacement and changes articulation; its score notes and arrivals stay fixed. |
| 3: harmonic support | Triangle, AD `$28`, SR `$58`, five-call release countdown; PWM disabled. | Test a less competing harmonic edge while retaining source pitch/chord cycles. Triangle backing previously lost useful ensemble vibrancy in Pink Panther; that prevents claiming it is a general improvement. Its role must be judged in this Rambo mix. |

Instrument-data offsets 0–13 hold frequency programs, 14–23 PWM, 24 control, 25 AD, 26 SR, 27 release countdown and 28 lifetime. The palette edits only those fields; duration-table offsets 32–48 stay intact. Shared banks receive voice-specific copies, and existing instrument-data pokes receive the same role choices. Tonal data controls deliberately remove SYNC/RING coupling while retaining GATE/TEST; explicit historical sound pokes remain unchanged. Source dramatic lead banks, including the delayed upward/downward sweeps, are explicitly checked rather than inferred from score identity.

**Preserve articulation roles as well as notes.** Applying the ordinary envelopes everywhere made the source's closing pitch programs continue silently: the final notes start at calls 10034–10035 (about 200.2 seconds), and their authored long release remains part of the ending after 205 seconds. This candidate therefore retains source AD, SR and release countdown together for complete patches whose source release nibble is `$D`–`$F`, including their existing lifetime extensions. Ordinary palette values in the table have that exception. The preparer checks the actual final-note entry values on all three voices and rejects a deliberately shortened final release. It also inspects individual envelope pokes in their executed source context; none belong to a long-release patch in this pinned graph. No added fade or note is used. The signal receipt requires rendered closing body at seconds 205–211, alongside the pre-clear decay checks and human ending comparison.

The [technique lookup](../../../docs/research/technique-index.md), [Rambo instrument card](../../../docs/research/composition/instruments/rambo-decoded-programs.md), [short-note guidance](../../../docs/research/composition/sound-design-repertoire.md#shape-short-note-endings-in-context) and [gesture listening findings](../../../docs/research/composition/gestures/README.md#listening-findings-that-guide-selection) informed these choices. Earlier Bach trials rejected odd short attacks and competing percussion; this treatment instead changes the ongoing body of existing roles. That precedent does not award this candidate a musical verdict.

## Reproduce and review

```sh
python3 scripts/setup-project.py --only references --include rambo-first-blood-part-ii
python3 scripts/listening-package.py config/listening/rambo-interpretation.json --output build/rambo-interpretation-v1
python3 scripts/publish-listening.py build/rambo-interpretation-v1/run.json --selection songs/studies/rambo-interpretation/audition.json --output build/rambo-interpretation-listening-v1
```

The [one recipe](../../../config/listening/rambo-interpretation.json) checks a complete normalized semantic ledger, independently compiles the edited numeric model over all 11,000 calls, verifies the complete callable SID in VICE, renders the original SID, faithful reconstruction and interpretation for 225 seconds, then prepares the two matched comparisons. Its preparation guard deliberately changes one source pitch, one literal row duration, one dramatic-slide delay and one final release to verify that the relevant identity checks reject them. CPU, VICE, renderer snapshots and listening claims remain separate.

The publisher generates these local files under `build/rambo-interpretation-listening-v1/`; they are not shipped downloads in Git. Open the generated `build/rambo-interpretation-v1/listening.md` for clickable local links.

- `faithful-then-interpretation-phrase.ab.wav`: source seconds 32–48, faithful reconstruction first, half-second gap, interpretation second; whole-mix AC RMS matching. Faithful gain is unity; candidate gain is 0.815229 (−1.774 dB).
- `faithful-then-interpretation-ending.ab.wav`: source seconds 205–225, same order and matching method; assess the release and final phrase in context. Faithful gain is unity; candidate gain is 0.671514 (−3.459 dB).
- `faithful-full.wav` and `interpretation-full.wav`: complete faithful reconstruction and complete interpretation at original renderer levels, no normalization.
- `original-source-full.wav`: complete original SID subtune 1, initialized and rendered with the same explicit PAL MOS6581 target.
- `original-source-phrase.wav` and `original-source-ending.wav`: separately labelled excerpts of the original-SID render, provided locally for reference; these are not the first halves of the interpretation A/B.

Listening question: **Does this palette make the melody sing and the bass feel solid while keeping useful accompaniment through the phrase and ending?** Compare the complete files before accepting a whole-song interpretation. A more obvious signal change is not necessarily a musical improvement.

The recipe retains clipping/energy checks and omission diagnostics for every role. Those control-mask renders measure signal contribution, not exact independent stems. Pre-clear windows inspect decay before each modeled final lifetime clear, rather than accepting the candidate solely because its last second is quiet. Modeled call times are not measured SID bus timestamps; the ending comparison remains necessary. Detailed receipts and actual comparison gains are linked in `build/rambo-interpretation-v1/listening.md` and `run.json`.

**Technical verification, 2026-10-15:** all 43,091 semantic score events and all 635/977/865 note starts retain their source timing. The interpreted CPU and complete VICE runs match 93,923 ordered stores through 11,000 calls and ten CPU state checkpoints; VICE's maximum cadence error is seven checkpoint cycles. Both 225-second full renders are unclipped, the corrected closing phrase retains rendered body and all three omission residuals have zero AC RMS in their pre-clear windows. The full candidate WAV SHA-256 is `e515cee07c7fb77120f0433198427f00e4f9e57a9f784d2492d4dc8cb3812704`. The unchanged final run reused all 16 recipe stages in 0.760 seconds. These checks prepared the audition; the subsequent listener feedback did not establish a musical improvement.
