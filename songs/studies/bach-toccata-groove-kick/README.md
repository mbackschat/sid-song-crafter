## No-difference diagnosis

The [retained signal check](../../../evidence/bach-groove-kick/audibility-check.json) verifies the submitted comparison and separates signal presence from perception. A diagnostic render selecting only voice 3 produces nonzero kick bodies (AC RMS about 1,056–1,132 PCM16 units) and tails (591–683), then zero AC RMS between hits. The full mixed passage changes overall AC RMS by only about 0.076 dB. These measurements establish a synthesized contribution, not its audibility or musical value; raw mixed-sample subtraction also includes possible timing/phase effects and must not be labelled an isolated kick stem.

The drum coincides with existing bass attacks and is concentrated at low pitch, so perceptual blending is plausible, but masking and playback response are not proven causes. Keep the preferred subtraction version: there is no demonstrated musical benefit to this addition. Before any further trial, choose an explicit perceptual contrast and verify actual envelope output and in-context contribution; do not treat a nominal low register as proof of convincing weight.
