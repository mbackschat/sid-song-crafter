This separate experiment tests sustained game-like propulsion through the accepted arrangement's running RH passage, source beats 45–60.5 and performed seconds 30.71–41.9725. The written foreground, all its onsets and durations, and the accepted lead sound remain unchanged. The original accepted full score is the reference; no Gyruss audio, program, extracted data or documentation was consulted to design this candidate.

The [editable score](score.json) keeps the existing offbeat A3 accompaniment, derived from the source's repeated LH A4, but limits each note to the lesser of 0.10 seconds and its original duration. A newly authored A2 eighth-note ostinato supports it, with longer quarter accents: 0.24-second strong notes alternate with 0.13-second weak notes. A2 is two octaves below the written A4; it is an arrangement addition, and there is no written pedal in this passage. Both accompaniment voices use clean pulse waves and short releases, without additional melodic lines or pitch effects. The [native recipe](hybrid.json) owns these two groove-only patches; the accepted instruments and their numbering remain unchanged.

The [preparation record](preparation.json) separates every shortened existing A3 note from each authored A2 note and retains its source basis. It checks foreground equality and equality of all score events outside the bounded window. Original sources and accepted inputs remain in the [full study](../bach-toccata-full/README.md). This is an accompaniment interpretation, not a source transcription or a Gyruss reconstruction.

The window includes the entry and the start of the following original-score passage; changed accompaniment releases and inactive register state persist beyond the intervention's score window, until their next original instrument selections around 43.7 seconds. Unity gain preserves the new accompaniment's balance change. No PCM identity outside the score window is claimed.

Executed native checks on 2026-10-14 verified all 923 candidate notes, against the reference's 895 notes, through 4,419 calls. Every foreground and shared filter/global post-call register remained equal. All 28 authored A2 notes have 4–11 full-pitch gated frames followed by 7–14 gate-off frames before their next attack; all 56 articulated A3 notes have 2–3 full-pitch body frames and 5–9 gate-off frames. These true gaps distinguish the groove from adjacent sustained same-pitch notes. Both standalone PRGs passed independent headless VICE checkpoints through call 4,400. The complete renders are 88.122925 seconds, mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz, with zero clipping and silent final tails. The [retained score, gate and signal checks](../../../evidence/bach-groove-blend/score-and-groove-check.json) record the measurements and their limits; musical acceptance remains separate.
