## Intent and arrangement

Aim for a playful, assertive synth groove with a clear hook and springy bass. Source beats 68–100 carry the hook; beats 100–132 switch to rhythmic chord stabs. Preserve that alternating structure rather than inventing continuous backing. The [reduction](reduction.json) retains 212 of 1,030 selected source notes: 46 melody, 66 upper chord tones and 100 bass notes. All 5,199 source notes remain in the private complete common score. Every selected omission is recorded in the generated adaptation report; retained pitches, onsets and durations are unchanged at this score layer.

The [candidate](native.json) reuses these three native programs byte-for-byte from the [project repertoire bank](../studies/sidwizard-repertoire/repertoire.json). The [plain reference](plain.json) renders the same selected notes with triangle tones. The accepted [Take On Me transfer](../../docs/research/composition/instruments/take-on-me-decoded-programs.md) is supporting evidence for separating lead motion from filtered bass.

Target: one MOS8580 at `$D400`, PAL, native SID-Wizard Extra, one update per frame. Original drums, lower chord tones and bass pitch bends are omitted. The 208 selected pitch-wheel events span −8192 to 0; no semitone range is inferred. Thus neither rendering is a lossless performance of the downloaded MIDI. The initial audition tests tonal character and balance; drum groove, full harmony and bends remain explicit arrangement decisions for the complete version.

## Verification, reuse and next step

The [aggregate receipt](audition-verification.json) contains fingerprints, counts and checks without source notes. Both exports pass the complete 212-start native schedule audit and 1,795 CPU calls. VICE checkpoints cover boot, the two musical sections and post-stop calls through 1,790. Both full WAVs are unclipped and have zero final-half-second AC RMS. This is emulated execution and signal evidence, not hardware playback or musical acceptance.

Native conversion reports zero timing adjustments and 45 articulation entries: 44 short-note gate releases delayed one frame to retain the full-pitch body, plus the general native onset/envelope limitation. These are distinct from unchanged numeric source durations. Adjacent notes can retain an envelope; the known tempo-1 first-frame behavior remains explicit. A quiet excerpt tail is not a complete-song ending verdict.

On 2026-10-15 the first five-stage run took **22.68 seconds**; an unchanged repeat took **0.24 seconds**, with all stages reused. The new work consists of source research and note-free import/reduction/palette/listening configuration. No new importer, player, renderer or orchestration script was required. Source selection and arrangement review took additional human/agent effort that was not reliably timed; these execution figures do not establish total productivity gain or a compelling musical result.
