## What “free” covers

Martin Galway's [release statement](https://github.com/MartinGalway/C64_music/blob/a458a3687e27a63647bbe41968094f0e6d0965a0/README.md) says that he acquired the rights from Infogrames, currently owns the released music and programming code, and permits reassembly, modification and new music with credit. The repository includes [GPLv3](https://github.com/MartinGalway/C64_music/blob/a458a3687e27a63647bbe41968094f0e6d0965a0/LICENSE). This is a positive adaptation and redistribution basis, unlike a free download from an archive. The inspected source revision is `a458a3687e27a63647bbe41968094f0e6d0965a0`; the [repository landing page](https://github.com/MartinGalway/C64_music) and [raw licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MartinGalway/C64_music/main/LICENSE) provide accessible primary alternatives when pinned GitHub pages fail to load.

Publish a resulting covered music package under GPLv3, retain attribution and licence notices, identify modifications and provide the preferred editable arrangement plus the source/scripts needed to build its SID and render its WAV. Do not label the music MIT merely because the surrounding tools are MIT. GPLv3 §2 makes coverage of output depend on its content; a recording of the covered composition is not cleared by calling it program output. The [publication-permission owner](game-music-permissions.md) and [repository boundary](../../CLAUDE.md#public-repository-boundary) retain the packaging rules; this shortlist does not authorize uploading original archive SIDs or reference WAVs.

## Search scope and limits

The bounded search used each title with `2SID`, `2 SID`, `dual SID`, `dual-SID` and `stereo SID`; explicit filename searches included `Wizball_2SID`, `Arkanoid_2SID` and `Game_Over_2SID`. It also used title-specific `site:csdb.dk` and `site:hvsc.de` queries, inspected indexed CSDb title/release metadata, the composer's source repository, Remix64, SLAY/STIL and Zophar. Some direct CSDb requests were blocked and official HVSC directory/text-file requests failed. This was an indexed web/catalogue check, not a full current HVSC header scan, executable audit or inspection of every scene release.

A supplementary scan of the user's local HVSC release 85 found **23 files whose names contain `wizball` or `arkanoid`**, including named covers and alternative drums. None declares a second or third SID in its PSID/RSID header. The [complete filename/hash/header receipt](../../evidence/stereo-candidates/local-hvsc-header-scan.json) retains that bounded result. Reproduce the selection with `rg --files /path/to/C64Music | rg -i '(wizball|arkanoid).*\.sid$'`; inspect second-chip byte `$7A` for header version 3 or later and third-chip byte `$7B` for version 4 or later. This does not cover renamed works, newer archives or undeclared hardware accesses, and is not an execution audit.

“No confirmed native two-SID arrangement found” is the strongest supported conclusion. Stereo MP3s, Amiga arrangements, live recordings and emulator voice panning do not establish an independently addressed two-chip C64 program. Conversely, the absence of a `2SID` filename does not rule one out. Resolve an identified native candidate's chip addresses and actual writes before claiming a first or unique rendition. Follow the [two-SID arrangement contract](sid/dual-sid.md#expand-the-musical-capacity-not-only-the-stereo-image) when either work is explicitly selected.

The same Galway release includes Game Over and Green Beret; they were not promoted into additional strong recommendations. Green Beret's header explicitly credits Konami as well as Galway, and its [STIL entry](https://www.slayradio.org/sidinfo/MUSICIANS%2FG%2FGalway_Martin%2FGreen_Beret.sid) flags further source material. Game Over offers the same published GPL basis, but this bounded pass did not establish a comparably strong recommendation or separately examine the provenance of its recorded drums. Other composers' well-known tunes remain subject to the [existing unresolved per-song findings](game-music-permissions.md#per-song-disposition); free playback or a GPL player does not license its repertoire.
