# LEAD-03 / PLUCK-01 — bright attack pluck

Design and measurement date: 2026-10-12. Evidence level: **rendered experiment**; the adapted high-register Für Elise patch has a separate [listening verdict](../../../songs/fuer-elise-native.md). This original keyboard-like pluck tests whether a brief saw transient improves articulation while a triangle body keeps the sustained spectrum restrained. Intended test register: A3–C5 in scientific pitch notation. The design follows the [LEAD-03 and PLUCK-01 briefs](../sound-design-repertoire.md).

The native [bright instrument](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/native/instruments/pluck-bright.swi), [soft reference](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/native/instruments/pluck-soft.swi), [candidate phrase](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/native/pluck-candidate.swm) and [reference phrase](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/native/pluck-reference.swm) are generated from the [original source](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/repertoire.json). No upstream example patch or melody was copied. Original project material has no separate license grant; the [fixture README](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/README.md) distinguishes it from Hermit's upstream player/exporter code.

Target: SID-Wizard 1.97 Extra, one MOS8580 at `$D400`, PAL 985248 Hz, one update per 19656-cycle frame, approximately 50.1245 Hz. Both variants use one independent voice, control `$0A`, hard-restart ADSR `$0F00`, playing ADSR `$0734` and initial waveform `$09`. Vibrato, detune, ring/sync, pulse programming and filter routing are disabled. Both waveform programs explicitly establish the played pitch on their first row, then select gated triangle `$11` with pitch NOP `$80` and detune NOP `$FF`, then end.

The only changed parameter is the first waveform-table command: gated triangle `$11` in the reference, gated saw `$21` in the candidate. The [exported candidate write log](../../../../build/instrument-trials/pluck-candidate/pluck-candidate.writes.json) confirms restart waveform `$09` at tick 2, saw `$21` at tick 3 and triangle `$11` at tick 4. The transient therefore lasts one PAL update, approximately 19.95 ms; the same transition recurs at later explicit selections. ADSR, body, restart policy and phrase bytes match exactly, and the complete frequency, ADSR and filter trajectories match the reference. The release nibble is 4 and there is no gate-off table override. Explicit instrument selections retrigger; the test's tied notes intentionally keep the existing decay/body and therefore may expose a useful limitation of plucked legato.

The phrase includes fast repeated notes, a longer hold, a rest/re-entry, tied notes, a change to the static pulse and a final higher pluck. In the mix it serves as a sparse answering/accompaniment voice; that pair also changes lead width motion and bass transient. The candidate's only waveform families before the later instrument change are saw and triangle; the reference contains triangle alone. Both final gates clear at tick 362. The [trial report](../../../../build/instrument-trials/trial.json) records 501 RAM-only py65 calls, init cost 1944 cycles, peak play cost 1269 of 19656 cycles and 3100-byte player/music payloads at `$1000`. Both libsidplayfp 3.1.1/SIDLite MOS8580 renders contain 441000 samples at 44100 Hz, peak magnitude 4532, zero clipping and final-half-second AC RMS zero. Full-file AC RMS is 836.042 for the reference and 836.103 for the candidate; their near-equal level does not establish whether the transient is musically useful. Model comparison and hardware behaviour remain untested; the CPU audit excludes interrupt/badline contention.

Reproduction uses the [generation and trial commands](../../../../songs/studies/sidwizard-repertoire/README.md). Listen to [the pluck A/B](../../../../build/instrument-trials/comparisons/pluck.ab.wav) and [the full mix A/B](../../../../build/instrument-trials/comparisons/mix.ab.wav); original-amplitude [reference](../../../../build/instrument-trials/pluck-reference/pluck-reference.wav) and [candidate](../../../../build/instrument-trials/pluck-candidate/pluck-candidate.wav) renders are retained. The [matching report](../../../../build/instrument-trials/comparisons/pluck.comparison.json) applies unity gain to A and 0.999927 (-0.000633 dB) to B using full-file AC RMS, including rests/tails. This original phrase has no isolated preference recorded. In the adapted higher-register Für Elise passage, the listener preferred the triangle-only onset; see the [bounded attack study](../../../songs/fuer-elise-native.md). Acceptance asks whether repeated notes gain definition without a distracting click or false pitch, whether the body remains audible during the held note, and whether the transient survives at mix level. Parameter and waveform checks do not answer those musical questions.
