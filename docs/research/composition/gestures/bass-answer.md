# GROOVE-01 — bass arrival and rhythmic answer

**Status:** original [Für Elise implementation](../../../../songs/studies/fuer-elise-interpretation/README.md) rendered; listening approval pending. The variations below remain design options unless implemented in that study. Browse the [gesture library](README.md).

## Musical purpose

Make a harmonic arrival feel grounded, then add a short answering gesture in a melodic gap. For an A-minor/E-major passage, try a low A root, a separated E fifth or octave response, and a rest before the next important melody entry. Keep a clear pulse; reserve pickups or shortened repetitions for selected returns instead of filling every gap.

Choose register by its role in the mix. The [DRAX study](../fuer-elise-reference.md) shows a first bass body near A1, below our baseline's A2. An original trial can place A at A1 while retaining E2 for the dominant. This is a deliberate arrangement choice, not a recovered source note or a guarantee of stronger bass on every speaker.

## SID realization and variations

Use one pulse voice, with sole ownership of a low-pass filter and an envelope that leaves an audible body after the attack. Compare plain pulse against a brief noise-to-pulse accent on important arrivals. Vary gate duration, rests and register before increasing resonance. Separate the accented bass from lighter answering notes so every onset does not become a drum hit.

The existing [triangle kick-to-bass patch](../instruments/bass-02-kick-to-bass.md) provides an editable sharing mechanism, but its isolated comparison produced little or no listener-perceived difference. A noise accent is a different, unreviewed candidate; it must earn its place in a mix.

## Costs and acceptance

Percussion temporarily consumes the bass oscillator; verify recovery to the intended root. The shared filter affects every routed voice, and 6581/8580 cutoff responses need separate evaluation. Short gates do not imply short release tails.

Accept when the root remains intelligible, the answer adds movement, and the next melodic entry stays clear. Retain a plain-bass version to detect masking or excessive attack. The [measured reference](../fuer-elise-reference.md) supplies mechanism evidence; phrase placement and the proposed root/fifth variation are original design decisions.
