## Musical criteria for our opening audition

These are our proposed criteria, inferred from the written reception and the trial's existing organ brief; they are not reports of hearing Gyruss or our candidate.

- **A decisive entrance:** the octave gestures should have audible authority and remain clear through rapid notes. The opening should establish character before the listener has to recognize the tune by pitch alone.
- **Harmonic body with three voices:** preserve the pedal foundation and the important upper/inner relationships through the sustained diminished passage. Document omitted notes. Judge whether the held texture has weight; audition any chord cycling specifically for whether it weakens that impression.
- **Energy with dramatic pacing:** runs should move purposefully, while the written pauses and fermatas create tension. Our organ-inspired treatment should retain those gestures even where a game-oriented adaptation values continuous motion.
- **Register and balance:** high notes should remain intelligible without dominating the pedal or making the sustained texture tiring. Envelope levels, static pulse width and any filter choice are musical candidates to evaluate, not settings inferred from these reviews.

Provide the matching organ-performance passage beside the raw single-SID render and ask the listener about entrance, sustained weight, harmonic clarity and pace. A future authorized Gyruss audio comparison would need the selected platform and passage named separately. Written expectations and actual musical acceptance remain different evidence.
