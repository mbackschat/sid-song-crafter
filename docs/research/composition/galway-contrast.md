# Galway contrast: Rambo loader and Arkanoid title

This bounded contrast checks three findings from the [Rambo loader study](rambo-preparation.md) against executed **Arkanoid title #1**. The result supports shared authoring ideas while retaining important engine constraints. It does not establish a universal Galway style, complete a second reconstruction or provide musical acceptance. The required [study-plan comparison](../../../PLAN.md#required-contrasting-references-for-the-six-composers) is complete for these bounded findings and the listening result below; no new instrument or gesture is promoted here.

## Three findings and their authoring consequences

| Testable Rambo finding | Executed Arkanoid result | Scope and authoring consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A score boundary or rest does not necessarily end ongoing oscillator programming. Rambo has separate SOUND state and lifetime, including modulation after top-level score return. | Voice 1 receives note 29 at refresh 128, then Rest `$5F` at 256 and 384. Its PWM body writes every refresh 138–511: 374 ordered low/high pairs. Every pair matches a two-stage program initialized at `$0800`, fifty steps of +10 followed by fifty of −10, with repeating counters and retained width. | **Supported across these two works for modulation during score rests.** A rest must remain separate from silence or instrument reset in an editable arrangement. Arkanoid's terminal lifetime behavior was not tested; Rambo's exact ending algorithm remains case-specific. |
| Score data can invoke machine-code hooks to change ongoing behavior. Rambo executes its hooks on physical voice 1, including a reentrant Morse-start refresh. | At refreshes 2304 and 5888, Arkanoid's physical voice 3 executes code-command dispatcher `$2A46` and indirect jump `$2A55` to `$374B` and `$3751`. Those hooks set RAM mode shadow `$3FFF` to `$10` and `$00`; the second also writes `$D417`. All 1,649 observed foreground restore instructions at `$41A0` use `shadow OR $0F`, producing `$1F` or `$0F`. | **Shared authoring idea, different engine contract.** Hook placement and meaning must be explicit, with saved shared filter state separate from the physical register. Arkanoid's other code-command slots statically point to `$0A5A/$0A5D`, outside its loaded payload, and the published source marks them unavailable; this is not a portable arbitrary-hook facility. Rambo's reentrant Morse mechanism does not transfer from this evidence. |
| The verified Rambo loader uses three oscillator programs and ordinary master volume, not an executed synthesized-drum volume stream. Its complete 87,534-store ledger contains only one `$D418` store, initialization value `$0F`. | Arkanoid's first 512 completed refreshes produce 22,045 SID stores, including 17,426 `$D418` stores. The foreground tom routine at `$42ED–$42F6` loads an accumulator, adds an immediate increment, retains the byte and writes its low nibble. The first complete 150-write tom body spans 15,398 checkpoint cycles; intervals within it range from 31 to 297 cycles. | **Different mechanism serving the additional percussion role documented in the published account.** This is computed volume-path audio alongside three oscillators, not a fourth oscillator or evidence of recorded sample data. A once-per-frame register scheduler cannot represent this resource/timing contract. Keep this synthesis path and its model assumptions separate from transferable oscillator instruments. |

The PWM prediction covers its actual opening state and instructions; it is not a new general player. The hook check covers the two selected title events through refresh 8191. That later trace records selected hook, mode-shadow and restoration observations, rather than every intervening SID write. The complete ordered SID-store ledger belongs to the opening capture only.

## Controlled drum diagnostic and listening

The causal experiment changes only archive byte `$42F1`, the operand of `ADC #$65`, to `$64` on an ignored copy. Both initialized full-machine runs complete the same 512 title refreshes. Each verifies all **2,400** executed tom writes against `(previous accumulator + increment) & $FF`, followed by `& $0F`. The intervention changes **2,250** volume values, while preserving every non-`$D418` store record and every SID writer PC, address, order and checkpoint time. This is a same-material synthesis diagnostic; it is not an independently recreated instrument, an improved arrangement or recorded sample playback. Timing equality is bounded to the observed checkpoints.

The [managed listening index](../../../build/arkanoid-corroboration/listening/listening.md) links the initialized six-second original, the six-second arithmetic diagnostic and **original first → 0.5-second silence → diagnostic** comparison at unity gain. Both PCM16 mono 44.1 kHz renders have the exact requested duration, nonzero AC signal and no rail samples. They use libsidplayfp 3.1.1/SIDLite, explicit subtune 1, PAL, declared MOS6581 and power-on delay zero. Renderer snapshots remain audio diagnostics, separate from the VICE ledger. The listening question is whether this single arithmetic change audibly changes the tom's percussive identity. On 2026-10-13 the listener answered “Sorry, no” when asked whether the percussion changed while the pitched layer stayed similar. Record this as **no audible change detected** for the submitted pair. The one-unit increment change has no demonstrated perceptual editing value from this trial. Executed volume differences remain established, while preferred timbre, hardware equivalence and musical usefulness of a new drum instrument remain unclaimed.
